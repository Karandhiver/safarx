Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Safar X application, we collect information that you provide to us directly, including but not limited to:

  • Personal identification information (Name, Mobile Number, Email Address).
  • Vehicle and licensing documentation (if provided for fleet logs).
  • Location data (GPS coordinates) to provide accurate routing and surge radar functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Provide real-time traffic and profit-routing suggestions.
  • Calculate your digital hisaab (earnings logs) locally on your device or securely in our cloud.
  • Notify you about beta access, waitlist status, and important platform updates.

3. Data Sharing and Security

Safar X does not sell your personal data to third-party data brokers. We implement strict, industry-standard security measures to ensure that your financial data and duty logs remain confidential and accessible only to you.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. If you wish to exercise these rights, please reach out to us via our Contact page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team via the Contact page.
